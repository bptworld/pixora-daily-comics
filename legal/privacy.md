# Pixora Daily Comics Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

## Who this applies to

PixoraHQ publishes Pixora Daily Comics. This policy covers the Android app and its browser preview. For privacy questions or requests, email info@pixorahq.com.

## Comic delivery

The Android app requests public comic listings and artwork from GitHub over HTTPS. The browser preview uses bundled copies and requests the same public feed when you refresh. GitHub receives standard network information, including your IP address, request time and client information, to serve those requests. GitHub handles its own logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

## Information stored on your device

The Android app remembers your selected series and saves the latest downloaded listing and cached artwork on your device. These files help the app load faster and show saved issues offline. You can remove them by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. The preview includes its starter comics in the HTML file.

## Accounts, analytics and permissions

Daily Comics has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The Android app uses internet access to load comics. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. PixoraHQ does not receive individual comic-reading histories through the app.

## Support and privacy requests

If you contact PixoraHQ, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use it to reply, investigate the issue and keep records as needed to handle the request or meet legal obligations. Contact info@pixorahq.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us.

## Provider privacy policies

GitHub General Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Google manages Google Play downloads and any Play services you choose to use under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
